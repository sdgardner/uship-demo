Frequently Asked Questions

How does uShip work?

Unlike traditional brokers or transport companies, uShip is an online shipping marketplace that connects users with thousands of professional and independent carriers on one platform. Fueled by 20 years of pricing data and a nationwide network of over 35,000 active carriers, we're able to provide transparent, competitive pricing that takes the guesswork out of big shipping.

Why do shipping prices vary on uShip?

Many factors can affect shipping prices, including distance, item size and weight, fuel costs, seasonality, and available truck space along a carrier's route. Since uShip is an online shipping marketplace, multiple carriers can submit quotes for the same shipment, giving you a range of pricing options to compare and helping you find a competitive price.

Is uShip legit and how are carriers vetted?

Yes, uShip is a legitimate online shipping marketplace that connects customers with independent carriers who compete for shipments. Instead of assigning a single shipping company, uShip gives you tools to review carriers, compare quotes, and choose the option that best fits your needs.

Is it safe to ship with uShip?

Absolutely! At uShip, trust and safety are our top priorities, and we offer several protective measures to ensure the security of your shipment and payment. Our comprehensive review system and partnership with SaferWatch make it easy to find reliable carriers who meet your standards for compliance and safety.

What happens after I accept a quote?

After you accept a quote on uShip, your shipment is booked, and you'll be connected directly with your chosen carrier. From your Booking Confirmation page, you can review shipment details, access the carrier's contact information, and coordinate pickup and delivery plans.

What happens if something goes wrong or my shipment is damaged?

Damage and theft during shipments booked on uShip are uncommon, but we still provide several layers of protection to help give you peace of mind. Many shipments are eligible for the uShip Protection Plan at checkout, which offers cargo protection in case your item is damaged, lost, or stolen during transit.

Is it better to ship your vehicle or drive it yourself?

It depends on the distance, your timeline, and how much time and effort you want to spend on the trip. Driving your vehicle yourself may seem cheaper at first, but expenses like fuel, lodging, meals, and wear and tear can add up quickly on long-distance moves.
