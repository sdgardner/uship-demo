Frequently Asked Questions

How does uShip work?

Unlike traditional brokers or transport companies, uShip is an online shipping marketplace that connects users with thousands of professional and independent carriers on one platform.

Fueled by 20 years of pricing data and a nationwide network of over 35,000 active carriers, we're able to provide transparent, competitive pricing that takes the guesswork out of big shipping. Simply tell us about the item you need to ship, compare quotes from carriers, and book the option that fits your needs and budget.

Why do shipping prices vary on uShip?

Many factors can affect shipping prices, including distance, item size and weight, fuel costs, seasonality, and available truck space along a carrier's route. Since uShip is an online shipping marketplace, multiple carriers can submit quotes for the same shipment, giving you a range of pricing options to compare and helping you find a competitive price.

You may see lower prices when a carrier already has space on a truck traveling your route and can add your shipment along the way. Larger, heavier, or time-sensitive shipments may cost more, and difficult routes or remote locations can also affect the cost. By comparing quotes from different carriers, you can choose the option that best fits your budget and timeline, all in one place on uShip.

Is uShip legit and how are carriers vetted?

Yes, uShip is a legitimate online shipping marketplace that connects customers with independent carriers who compete for shipments. Instead of assigning a single shipping company, uShip gives you tools to review carriers, compare quotes, and choose the option that best fits your needs.

Carrier profiles on uShip include important information like verified customer reviews, service policies, and insurance details so you can evaluate carriers before booking. Many carriers also choose to participate in compliance monitoring through our partner SaferWatch, which helps track safety and regulatory information.

Because multiple carriers can submit quotes for your shipment, you can compare pricing, read verified feedback from past customers, and select a carrier you feel confident working with. Secure checkout through uShip Payments also keeps transactions cashless and protected.

Is it safe to ship with uShip?

Absolutely! At uShip, trust and safety are our top priorities, and we offer several protective measures to ensure the security of your shipment and payment.

Our comprehensive review system and partnership with SaferWatch make it easy to find reliable carriers who meet your standards for compliance and safety.

When it's time to pay, uShip Payments is our secure, cashless system that keeps your funds safe until you give the green light. Plus, our uShip Protection Plan offers comprehensive cargo protection at checkout, delivering peace of mind at a competitive rate.

And when you need a helping hand, our experienced support team is ready to tackle any questions or concerns you may have.

At uShip, we're not just a shipping platform – we're your trusted ally in navigating the sometimes choppy waters of big shipping. Visit the Help Center for more tips and best practices to protect your shipment.

What happens after I accept a quote?

After you accept a quote on uShip, your shipment is booked, and you'll be connected directly with your chosen carrier. From your Booking Confirmation page, you can review shipment details, access the carrier's contact information, and coordinate pickup and delivery plans.

From there, you can message your carrier through the uShip platform to confirm logistics, share contact information, and answer any remaining questions before pickup.

Payment is completed through uShip Payments, our secure, cashless system. After pickup, you may also be able to track shipment progress when carriers opt into location sharing.

As always, if you need help at any point, uShip Customer Support is here to help.

What happens if something goes wrong or my shipment is damaged?

Damage and theft during shipments booked on uShip are uncommon, but we still provide several layers of protection to help give you peace of mind. Many shipments are eligible for the uShip Protection Plan at checkout, which offers cargo protection in case your item is damaged, lost, or stolen during transit.

Carriers on the uShip marketplace may also carry their own insurance, and carrier profiles often include insurance information so you can review coverage before booking.

In addition, uShip offers the Ship with Confidence Guarantee. If you follow our Safe Shipping Guide and a service provider fails to complete the job after payment, uShip may reimburse unrefunded payments up to $500. If your item is damaged, lost, or stolen and you're unable to recover costs through the Protection Plan or the carrier's insurance, the guarantee may also provide compensation up to $500.

If an issue does occur, uShip's support team is available to help guide you through the next steps, including filing a claim or resolving payment disputes.

Is it better to ship your vehicle or drive it yourself?

It depends on the distance, your timeline, and how much time and effort you want to spend on the trip. Driving your vehicle yourself may seem cheaper at first, but expenses like fuel, lodging, meals, and wear and tear can add up quickly on long-distance moves.

Shipping your vehicle can be a convenient alternative, especially if you're relocating, buying or selling a car, or managing a multi-vehicle move. Through uShip's online shipping marketplace, you can compare quotes from multiple car carriers, review customer feedback, and choose the option that fits your timeline and budget.

In some cases, carriers may already be traveling along your route and have available space on their trailer, which can make shipping your vehicle more cost-effective than driving it yourself.
